Workshop on common probability distributions

Applying a distribution through visualization, simulation and estimation

I am an engineering apprentice in electronic systems, tele­communications and computer science at the engineering school of the Conser­vatoire national des arts et métiers (CNAM). As part of my studies, I work at the research and develop­ment laboratory EDF R&D (Chatou).

About this work

This work was carried out under the supervision of Dariush GHORBANZADEH (CNAM). Its aim is to represent, simulate and apply common probability distri­butions to experimental data. Ten probability distri­butions were selected.

The theoretical properties of each distri­bution are presented in this work. A human–machine interface then makes it possible to simulate (reproduce) the desired distri­bution from its para­meters, which gives an idea of its behavior as its para­meters vary. Para­meter estimation is a key step in the appli­cation part: we used the method of moments together with the gradient method.

For the appli­cation, the interface lets you select an external file. The files should be plain text with a single column. As an illus­tration, a collection of data sets can be downloaded here.
