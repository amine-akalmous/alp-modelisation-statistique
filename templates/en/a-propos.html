Summer 2026 · Bangkok University, BU-CROCCS

Signal Modulation for Visible Light Communication (VLC) on Field-Programmable Gate Array (FPGA)

Pulse-position modulated (4-PPM) signals are generated on an FPGA and emitted by an LED. The rolling shutter of the CMOS sensor turns time into space: by measuring widths in pixels on the image, the transmitted data are recovered.

I presented the decoding algorithm and the frame structure at Bangkok University, under the supervision of Dr Poompat Saengudomlert and Dr Karel Sterckx.

2024 · Final-year project · Air France Industries KLM E&M

A charge management bench for the Boeing 777

Within the Industrial General Management, I designed and built a charging station for the BPAS, an avionics electronic unit that assists the opening of the passenger doors and the deployment of the evacuation slides on the Boeing 777.

Circuit board, embedded software and monitoring interface were designed together: automated 24-hour charging, real-time monitoring of each battery, supervision and alerts, in compliance with aviation safety requirements.
